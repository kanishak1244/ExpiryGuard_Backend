To keep our billing process transparent, this page outlines exactly what categories of information Dawaiflow handles, why we collect them, how they are processed, and how you can manage your data.

🔑 1. Pharmacist Account Data

What it is
Pharmacist username, secure account password (encrypted using bcrypt), and registered email address.
Why needed
To authenticate login sessions, secure portal access, and send real-time system alerts.
How used
Verified during credential entry. Authorizes the device to request inventory data.
Shared
No. Stored strictly within our secure database.
Deletion
Emailing deletion request to vashistkanishak9@gmail.com deletes the account record.

📦 2. Pharmacy Inventory & Catalog

What it is
Medicine names, manufacturer details, batch numbers, pack sizes, shelf locations, supplier logs, and current stock counts.
Why needed
To track active stock, alert you of expiring medicines, and suggest restocking quantities.
How used
Updated automatically on billing creations, returns, or manual inventory imports.
Shared
No. Inventory lists are private to each registered pharmacy.
Deletion
Can be purged by deleting the account or using inventory clear actions in your portal dashboard.

💳 3. Sales & Invoice Transcripts

What it is
Invoice numbers, purchase totals, payment modes, GST amounts, item quantities, and transaction timestamps.
Why needed
To generate printable retail receipts (PDF invoices) and construct dashboard business graphs.
How used
Aggregated inside your dashboard stats page to show total business values.
Shared
No. Sales metrics are private to your business.
Deletion
Associated sales records are purged upon request or account termination.

📸 4. Camera Label Scans

What it is
Temporary photos of medicine packaging labels containing textual product detail.
Why needed
To recognize medicine packaging information, matching brand names, pack sizes, batches, and expiries instantly without manual lookup.
How used
Processed in memory to extract text information. Images are discarded immediately after recognition analysis.
Shared
Transmitted securely via secure API to Google Gemini models for text parsing. Photos are not used for public model training.
Deletion
Not applicable, as images are not stored permanently.

👤 5. Customer & Supplier Contact Details

What it is
Customer names, mobile numbers entered during checkout, and supplier contact logs.
Why needed
To print customer details on invoices (compliant with standard billing formats) and manage supplier lists.
How used
Appended directly to the specific invoice record and stored in the database.
Shared
No. Customer databases are strictly private and are not shared with external advertising agents.
Deletion
Can be modified or deleted directly from your dashboard sales lists.
