The Counter Reality

The problem isn’t billing. It’s doing it twice.

Running a Pharmacy Shouldn’t Feel This Manual.

At a busy pharmacy counter, a customer waits while a pharmacist writes a bill by hand. Later, the same information has to be entered again into desktop billing software.

Manual billing, re-entering medicine information, difficult inventory tracking, and human errors steal hours of your time and lead to expiry-related losses.

Do that 50, 100, or 200 times a day, and a few extra steps quickly become hours of repetitive work.

Current Workflow Double Data Entry
  • 👥 1. Customer arrives at counter
  • ✍️ 2. Pharmacist writes paper bill by hand
  • 💊 3. Hand over medicines & collect cash/UPI
  • 🔄 4. Sit at desktop computer & re-type entire bill
  • 📊 5. Manually adjust software inventory counts
Dawaiflow Workflow One-Touch Automation
  • 👥 1. Customer arrives at counter
  • 📸 2. Take one photo of the medicine packages
  • 🤖 3. AI identifies medicines, batches & MRP automatically
  • 💳 4. Tax bill generated instantly with GST calculation
  • 📦 5. Inventory stock numbers update immediately
