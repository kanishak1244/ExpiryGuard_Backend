Privacy Policy

Dawaiflow (Powered by ExpiryGuard)
Last Updated & Effective Date: August 30, 2026

1. Information Collected

We collect various types of information to provide, secure, and improve our AI-powered pharmacy billing service.

Information Provided by Users

  • Account Data: Username, account password (hashed securely), and pharmacist email address.
  • Pharmacy Profile: Pharmacy/store name, business location details, contact number, and optionally your GSTIN (Goods and Services Tax Identification Number) for invoice printing.

Pharmacy & Business Operational Data

  • Inventory & Products: Stock counts, active batches, expiry dates, pack sizes, and manufacturer names.
  • Billing & Sales Records: Invoice totals, transaction logs, payment methods, GST breakdowns, and purchased medicine listings.
  • Customers & Suppliers: Optional customer name and phone numbers entered to print retail invoices, and supplier contact details.

2. Automatically Collected Information & Device Permissions

When you use our service, technical information is automatically logged, and specific device features are accessed:

  • Camera Permission: Required to capture images of medicine package labels. These photos are processed by our backend image-recognition algorithms to extract brand, batch, and expiry details. Photos are used for active scanning and are not saved permanently unless explicitly needed for debug logs.
  • IP Address & Cookies: We collect standard network headers, IP addresses, and secure authentication session cookies to keep you logged into the billing portal.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information strictly for business administration purposes:

  • To generate retail counter invoices and GST calculations on demand.
  • To automatically adjust inventory levels when a bill is created or a return is processed.
  • To alert pharmacists about low stock thresholds and expiring batches.
  • To analyze overall sales trends and demand to generate reorder alerts.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your account credentials, pharmacy catalog, active inventory, and sales histories are stored in a secure relational database (PostgreSQL) hosted by Supabase. Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt hashing before storage. We utilize industry-standard SSL/TLS transmission protection for all API communication.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your operational data or personal information. Data is shared with backend utility services only as necessary to run the app:

  • Supabase: Database and hosting provider.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used to send real-time alerts and stock warnings to devices.
  • Google Gemini API: Image files are processed through the Gemini API under secure configurations to identify medicine packaging text.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

During our free pilot phase, we retain your data (sales histories, product catalog) indefinitely to construct analytics summaries and verify system reliability. You can request the complete deletion of your account and all associated pharmacy/billing records at any time by emailing us at `vashistkanishak9@gmail.com`. We will process your deletion request within 14 business days.
